# ChatGPT

ChatGPT connects to MCP servers as custom connectors in **developer mode**.

1. In ChatGPT, open **Settings → Apps & Connectors → Advanced settings** and turn on **Developer mode**.
2. Back in **Apps & Connectors**, choose **Create**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Name:** `Pratinidhi India Toolkit`
   - **MCP server URL:** `https://mcp.pratinidhi.unitycodelabs.com/mcp`
   - **Authentication:** OAuth
4. Create the connector. A Pratinidhi page opens: enter your email, open the sign-in link we send, and choose
   **Allow**. Your first sign-in creates your account and wallet.
5. In a new chat, enable the connector and ask, for example, *"Split ₹10,000 at 18% GST from Maharashtra to
   Karnataka."*

Developer mode and its menus are OpenAI's and can change; if these steps don't match, search OpenAI's help for
"developer mode" and "MCP connectors". Which ChatGPT plans include it is up to OpenAI.

When a paid tool needs more credit, the answer includes a top-up link and nothing is charged for that call.
